How to make a track cover

Sizes, free tools and mistakes — briefly. Plus how to embed the image into MP3.

Embed a cover in MP3 →

Sizes

  • 3000×3000 — streaming standard.
  • 1080×1920 — stories and reels.
  • 1400×1400 — Apple Music minimum.

Where to make it

  • Canva — “Album cover” templates, 5 minutes.
  • Figma — more control.
  • Photopea — free Photoshop in the browser.

Common mistakes

  • Tiny text — unreadable on phones.
  • Someone else’s photos without rights.
  • Logos on covers for streaming.

FAQ

What size is a track cover?

3000×3000 pixels — the streaming standard. Minimum 1400×1400.

Where can I make a cover for free?

Canva, Figma or Photopea — free templates are enough.
