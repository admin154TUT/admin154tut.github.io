Download a Suno track by link

Paste a track link and get the file in Telegram. Works on phone and desktop, no extensions.

How it works

  1. Open the track in Suno → Share → copy the link.
  2. Paste it here and press “Check”.
  3. Press “Download in the bot” — the file arrives in Telegram.

What you get

  • MP3 320 with cover and tags.
  • Uncompressed WAV.
  • Lyrics.
  • Vertical video 1080×1920.

FAQ

How do I download a Suno track by link?

Paste the track link, press “Check”, then open the bot — the file arrives in Telegram.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — it’s a web tool: no extension needed, works in any browser.

What if the link is short?

Open the track in Suno, press Share → Copy link and paste it here — it will contain the track id.
