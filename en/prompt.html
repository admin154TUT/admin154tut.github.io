How it works

  1. Open a track in Suno → Share → copy the link.
  2. Send it to @SunoPromptHelperBot.
  3. Get a TXT: style + lyrics.

Why extract the prompt

  • Reproduce the style on a new track.
  • Make a series in the same sound.
  • Understand which tags work.
  • Move the track to another AI.
