The foundation

  • Drums — kick, snare, hi-hat.
  • Bass — low end and groove.
  • Melody — chords or a loop.

Tempo by genre

  • Trap — 130–150 BPM.
  • Boom bap — 80–95 BPM.
  • House — 120–130 BPM.
  • Lo-fi — 60–80 BPM.
