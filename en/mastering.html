How to mix and master a track

Balance, frequencies and loudness — three steps to make a track sound finished.

Get WAV for mastering →

Mixing

  • Balance levels: vocals louder, bass low.
  • Cut excess frequencies with EQ.
  • Add light compression.

Mastering

  • Tonal balance (EQ).
  • Compression and limiter.
  • Check on phone and headphones.

Common mistakes

  • Overloaded low end — rumble.
  • Too loud — a brick.
  • Mastering MP3 — artifacts.

FAQ

What is mixing?

Balancing instrument levels and frequencies so everything is audible.

What is mastering?

Final processing: loudness, tonal balance, limiter.

Why WAV for mastering?

Uncompressed WAV is cleaner than MP3 and adds no artifacts.
