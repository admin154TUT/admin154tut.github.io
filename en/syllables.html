Syllable counter for song lyrics

Paste your lyrics — see syllables per line and where the rhythm breaks.

Why it helps

  • Syllables = rhythm. Even lines sound cleaner.
  • 6–10 syllables is a comfortable vocal length.
  • Very different lines break the melody.

Tips

  1. Even out the lines in a verse.
  2. The hook can be shorter.
  3. Read it out loud — breaks are audible.
