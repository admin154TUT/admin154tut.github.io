How to write song lyrics

Structure, rhymes and chorus — no music theory needed. Plus how to turn the lyrics into a track.

Make a track from lyrics →

Structure

  • Verse — a story or detail, 4–8 lines.
  • Chorus — the main idea, 2–4 lines, repeated.
  • Bridge — contrast, 4–8 lines.

Rhymes

  • Perfect rhymes sound simple.
  • Near rhymes sound modern.
  • Internal rhymes inside a line.

Line length

  • Keep 6–10 syllables. Long lines break the rhythm.

FAQ

What are the parts of a song?

Verses, a chorus and a bridge. The chorus repeats, verses tell the story.

Do rhymes have to be perfect?

No — near and internal rhymes sound more modern.

How do I check the lyrics?

Read them out loud: rhythm problems are audible instantly.
