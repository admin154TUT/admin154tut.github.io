How to create music with AI

Beats, background, video, game and podcast music. Step by step, no instruments or theory.

Create and download →

Step 1. Pick the task

  • Beat — rhythm and bass, the base of a track.
  • Background — neutral music for video and voiceover.
  • Game music — loops that don’t get boring.
  • Podcast music — intro and stings.

Step 2. Describe the style

Genre, instruments, tempo and mood. For background — no vocals, no sharp drops.

Build the description in 10 seconds: prompt generator.

Step 3. Generate

  1. Open Suno and paste the prompt.
  2. Generate 2–3 takes.
  3. Pick the most even one in volume.

Step 4. Get the file

Send the link to @sunoMP3bot — get uncompressed WAV (for editing) or MP3 with tags.

FAQ

Can I create music with AI for free?

Yes, Suno has a free limit. The file is downloaded via the bot.

Is it good for video?

Yes, instrumental without vocals is convenient for editing and podcasts.

How do I get WAV?

Choose WAV in the bot — 44.1 kHz / 16-bit, uncompressed.
