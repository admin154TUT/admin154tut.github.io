How to create a song with AI

Full guide: idea → lyrics → style → generation → file. No music theory required.

Create and download →

Step 1. Pick an idea

Define three things: what the song is about, the mood, and the genre. The more specific, the better the result.

  • About: a story, an emotion, a character, a place.
  • Mood: warm, sad, angry, dreamy.
  • Genre: pop, rock, rap, lo-fi, jazz, electronic.

Step 2. Write lyrics

Simple structure: verse → chorus → verse → chorus → bridge. Keep lines short, repeat the key phrase.

Step 3. Choose the style

Describe genre, instruments and tempo — that's the prompt. Build it in 10 seconds with the prompt generator.

Step 4. Generate in Suno

  1. Open Suno and paste the prompt + lyrics.
  2. Press generate.
  3. Pick the better of the two takes.

Step 5. Get the file

Send the track link to the Telegram bot @sunoMP3bot — get MP3 320 with cover and tags, WAV, lyrics and video for Reels.
